# Murmur

Free dictation for your Mac. Double-tap **fn**, talk, and your words appear wherever you're typing.
Everything runs on your Mac: nothing you say is sent anywhere.

## Download

Get the latest **Murmur-x.y.dmg** from [Releases](https://github.com/jrtutor/murmur/releases/latest),
open it, and drag Murmur into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS will say it can't be
opened: go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. The welcome window
walks you through the rest.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later, plus about 5 GB of free space for
the speech models it downloads the first time.

## Updates

Murmur 1.1 and later update themselves from this page. Each update is signed, and Murmur only installs
updates that carry that signature.

This repository only holds the downloads.
