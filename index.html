Set it up
in a few minutes.

Murmur isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks you to approve it once. That step is normal for this app.

  1. Download Murmur and drag it into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it can't be opened. Click Done.
  3. Approve it. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Follow the welcome window. It walks you through microphone and Accessibility access, the 🌐 key setting, and a one-time speech model download of about 4 GB.

Questions.

How much is it?

It's free. There's no subscription and no account, and there are no servers to pay for.

Which Macs does it run on?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later, with about 5 GB of free space. 16 GB of memory is best. With 8 GB, turn AI Cleanup off.

When does it use the internet?

For two things: downloading its speech models the first time you open it, and checking for signed updates.

I already use another dictation app.

If it listens to fn too, quit it first so they don't both start.

What languages does it support?

English.
